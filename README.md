# Behavior Based Supplier Segmentation for Procurement Optimization

## Project Overview
This project focuses on identifying groups of suppliers that exhibit similar operational and financing behaviour patterns. Using Machine Learning-based clustering techniques, suppliers are segmented based on their performance across key business dimensions to support data-driven procurement decisions.
The objective is to help procurement leadership understand supplier behaviour and proactively apply targeted playbooks, financing limits, monitoring strategies, and supplier education programs before operational exceptions escalate.

## Business Problem
Leadership wants a data-led picture of which suppliers behave similarly on:

* Timing Behaviour
* Invoice Quality
* Dispute Cadence
* Corridor Mix

The insights generated from supplier segmentation can be used to optimize procurement operations, reduce risk, improve supplier relationships, and enhance financing efficiency.

## Project Objectives
* Analyze supplier behavioural patterns.
* Identify groups of suppliers with similar characteristics.
* Build a behaviour-based supplier segmentation framework.
* Provide actionable business insights for procurement teams.
* Enable proactive supplier management and risk mitigation.

## Supplier Segments Identified

The analysis identified four distinct supplier segments:

### 1. Cross-Border High-Risk Suppliers

* Higher dispute rates
* Lower invoice quality
* Higher cross-border activity
* Increased operational risk

### 2. Operationally Efficient Suppliers

* Fast funding cycles
* High invoice quality
* Low dispute rates
* Strong operational efficiency

### 3. Strategic Large-Value Suppliers

* High-value supplier relationships
* Strong invoice quality
* Low dispute rates
* Strategic procurement importance

### 4. Broad-Network Operational Suppliers

* Diversified supplier operations
* Broad buyer relationships
* Balanced operational performance


## Key Findings
* Operationally Efficient Suppliers and Strategic Large-Value Suppliers exhibited the most similar behaviour patterns.
* Cross-Border High-Risk Suppliers emerged as the most distinct segment due to slower funding cycles, lower invoice quality, higher dispute rates, and greater cross-border activity.
* Behaviour-based segmentation provides more actionable insights than traditional geography-based classifications.


## Business Impact
The proposed segmentation framework enables procurement teams to:

* Apply targeted supplier management playbooks
* Establish segment-specific financing limits
* Design focused supplier education programs
* Monitor supplier risk proactively
* Improve operational efficiency
* Reduce disputes and documentation exceptions

## Project Outcome
A complete behaviour-based supplier segmentation solution was developed using K-Means Clustering. The final framework provides procurement leadership with a data-driven understanding of supplier behaviour and supports proactive decision-making before operational exceptions escalate.
